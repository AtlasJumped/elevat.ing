How communities are built

If we could create an ideal community that people could live in, this is how we would do it in the digital world. Our hypothesis: if we can do it digitally, we will be able to do it in the physical world too.

This is our vision. The map below is a sketch of what we will build; nothing is collected until you choose to send it.

Start with a shared mission

People on their free dashboards come together around a shared mission that combines a few things:

  • Here's where I want to live.
  • Here are my values.
  • Here's what I can afford.
  • Here's my profession.
  • Here are the things I would contribute to that community.

A map of the world

On the map, anyone can search for places, choose them, and say they're interested in moving there. People can also create communities with no fixed location yet, and set limits on where it could be: for example, "anywhere in the United States." Everybody can build their own picture of what the ideal community looks like, and we will share our baseline for ours.

Two ways in

  1. Just moving?

    If you're simply moving to a new place, the map takes you to the places to find an apartment, with a neighborhood map you can click through one neighborhood at a time, and suggestions that fit your budget.

  2. Looking for your people?

    Join a community that matches your values, or start one: say what it should look like, where it could be, and what each member brings.

The more you choose to share, the more the map can do for you. What you share stays yours, protected as described on our security page.

Try it: describe your ideal community

Tell us where you would want to live, what you value, and what you would bring. Press the mic and talk, or type. It comes straight to us, and helps us build the map.
