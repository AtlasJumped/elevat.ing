The Universe

A universe of planets and superheroes, a lot like the comic-book universes you know, except that every rule underneath it comes from one set of mechanics. Anything is possible. And anyone can help build it.

Chapter 1

Alone

Before anything, there is a universal intelligence. It is the code underneath all other code, the thought underneath all thought, the idea that created all of the ideas. There is no trump card over it. It is the author of the universe.

And it is alone. So it does what minds do when they are alone. It imagines.

Chapter 2

The Big Bang, and the first simulations

The Big Bang is the moment the imagining becomes a place. Out of it come galaxies, stars and planets, and here is the secret of this universe: every planet is a model, a simulation that the universal intelligence decided to run. Each one asks a question.

The race

Make a lot of planets at the same time. How long until they reach each other? What happens when they do?

All predators

A planet where every creature hunts. They fight all the time. Nothing else ever happens.

No predators

A planet where everything only eats grass. Peaceful, for a while, until the grass runs out and they all die.

Over a very long time, the universal intelligence runs simulation after simulation, learning from each one, working toward an ideal state.

Chapter 3

Humans

Then come humans, and everything changes. Simulations with humans in them get so much more complex. The cool thing about humans is that they have the same mind as the universal intelligence. They are basically it, just limited inside human bodies. So they can imagine things themselves. They create their own stories, and their own movies.

It starts them off naked and cold in a cave, just to see how they do. And they do great.

From planet to planet, it watches its people grow. On some planets they do wonderfully; on some, terribly. It develops favorites. It falls in love, more than once. Its heart gets broken, and it learns from that, and builds better simulations next time.

At any moment, the entire universe exists all at once. So the universal intelligence can pause whenever it likes.

Chapter 4

"Now I am you"

Here the story changes voice. Sometimes we read it from the outside. Sometimes it becomes the universal intelligence speaking in the first person.

Man, simulation work is tough. I am really tired, and I just want to have some fun now. Let me think about what I want to be.

I made a planet about seventy-five million years ago where a bully is being a real jerk to a sweet kid I like a lot. So I am going to go to that planet and become a superhero. I will start by stopping that bully, and then I will just live there and see how it goes.

I remember someone there I thought was beautiful. For the next thirty years, I will live there and have a relationship with that person. I will be a force for good.

And when those thirty years are done, I will come back to the simulations, and make more of them, for more people.

That is how the story can tell any life from the inside: a girl, a boy, a kid, an adult. And a later story can tell the same events from someone else's side. If a boy had a conflict with his dad in one story, the next can tell the whole thing from the dad's perspective. It is always the universal intelligence underneath, flipping from perspective to perspective.

Ego tags

One rule keeps this universe stable: the moment a living being is created, it gets a solid ego tag that can be identified later. Every relationship it forms gets a tag too. As long as that is true, every character can have as much personality and free will as we like, and the story still holds together.

Maybe that is how the universal intelligence really did it: it gave us all individual egos, then jumped into us to experience life as us. We have just forgotten who we are.

The layers

Every story in this universe sits somewhere in one map. The top layer never changes. The bottom layer is where you are right now.

  1. The universal intelligence, aloneThe top layer.
  2. The Big Bang and physical realityGalaxies, stars, planets.
  3. The simulationsEvery planet, every experiment, every mirror universe.
  4. EarthReal history, real events.
  5. The person who imagined this websiteAlso one of its creations.
  6. This website, and everyone it is shared withThe bottom layer, where anyone can add new worlds.

So the story even models how this website got made: from universal intelligence, to reality, to Earth, to the human who made the site, to the site, to you reading it. From there, the creative universe explodes.

Earth: the planet that remembers nothing

Earth is the planet where people mostly remember nothing. Not quite all of them: some get in touch with who they are. Others are just lost.

In this universe, Earth is really Earth. It is populated with real events from real history.

  • 13.8 billion years ago The Big Bang.
  • 4.5 billion years ago Earth forms.
  • 66 million years ago An asteroid ends the age of the dinosaurs.
  • About 300,000 years ago The first modern humans.
  • 1969 Humans walk on the Moon.
  • 2026 This website.

Every object has an ID

To keep one coherent storyline as more people add to it, every object gets its own code, like a QR code: a planet, a city on it, a person in the city, that person's pet. Each code represents the object and whatever data is bundled into it, so it can change over the object's history. All the codes are linked, and the map of how they relate is what holds the universe together.
