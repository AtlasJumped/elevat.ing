# elevat.ing

The main site of our system of elevating websites (the elevat.ing family of websites). It took over the hub content that was on hmrfamily.life, without HMR material and without any personal details.

- `index.html`: caption, Thanksgiving banner, major-mission cards (thumbs and "Add your voice" on each), flagship sites, every site with its plan (`data/sites.json`, filtered from hmrfamily.life/x/domains.json: no HMR names, no prices, no sale wording), plans in depth, the vision (with a short animation), first-visit mission statement.
- `end-suffering/`, `security/`, `communities/`: plans in depth.
- `family/`, `family-health/`, `universe/`: separate pages, unlinked for now.
- `css/elv8.css`, `js/elv8.js`: shared style and page script. The bottom bar loads from hmrfamily.life/js/hmr-dock.js.
