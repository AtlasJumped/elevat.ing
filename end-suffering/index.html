Ending world suffering

Websites need to facilitate the decrease of human suffering globally. This is the page where we plan how.

This is a plan. None of it is running yet. Every step below is something we intend to build, in the open, for free.

The three things we want to end

🍎

Global starvation

Food that would be thrown away reaches the places that need it, fast.

🌱

Global poverty

Free tools, free access, and things people no longer need reaching people who do.

🏠

Global homelessness

Communities that form around shared values and what each person can contribute.

The idea: waste is lost work

In order to alleviate suffering, we have to minimize the amount of waste human beings have. We view the universe as a system of productivity. The universe itself forged the raw materials, and that took time. Human beings did the same thing when they put their innovation on those materials.

So anything the universe has created, we want to alter as little as we can, unless it achieves our purpose. And when human beings have put our innovation on it, we want to ensure that that innovation is not lost, and that somebody else can use it.

The actionable plan

  1. Give people an easy way to pass things on

    We need a way for people who have things they don't need anymore to give them to other people. Everybody gets a personal marketplace: take a picture, press the mic, and talk about it. No forms, no subscriptions, no spam. By default, anything that doesn't find a new home within a month becomes free.

  2. Know what is up for transfer

    Every item that people and companies are ready to let go of is listed in one place, with what it is, where it is, and what shape it's in.

  3. Figure out the demand

    We have the supply from the people. Then there is the demand from across the world. Somebody needs to figure out which places need what, and how much. We will map it, and keep the map open.

  4. Work out the logistics

    How much will it cost to ship these things, how fast can they get there, and which route makes sense. If a region needs a lot of food, we need to work on how to get food there quickly.

  5. Get surplus food moving early

    Today, companies often wait until food is almost rotten before they throw it out, and by then it can't be shipped. That is the problem we need to overcome. We want companies encouraged to hand over food they are going to throw out as soon as possible. Every moment it takes to get fruits and vegetables from a place where they are not needed to a shipping place is critical.

    Ideally the food stays fresh, not frozen. But if freezing is what it takes to get the nutrition to people and solve world hunger, we are happy to do that too. We will look up the value of each choice and show our numbers.

  6. Partner with volunteers and stores

    Volunteer crews, trucks and sorting, working with stores so that food which would be wasted feeds hungry people, and dogs too.

  7. Get everyone online

    Once people in third world countries have a computer that can reach the free dashboard, and the internet, they can say what they need and take part in all of this themselves.

  8. Report openly

    We think transparency is a key part of human innovation moving forward. We will show what moved, where it went, and what we learned, so anyone can check our work and improve it.

Why we think it can work

If our children are raised well, we treat animals better, we take care of our physical and mental health, we all save each other time, we work the jobs we are meant to be working, and we share and coach each other, then humanity will be elevated to such a point that we'll all want to help each other.

Our goal in ending global suffering is to make life in the richest countries so good, so free and so easy that we all end up banding together and saying: it's not fair for our lives to be this good while others are suffering. Let's fix it.
