Free hiring and recruitment for every company

Our goal is to optimize the hiring and recruitment process for every hiring manager and every company across the world, for free, forever, for both sides.

The plan
  • Add your resume and say whether you are open to work.
  • A hiring manager pastes the link to a public job posting. We show the exact search filter we will use, and it can never filter on years of experience or whether you are employed right now.
  • People whose resumes match get a link to one clean application: a yes or no next to each line of the job, and a mic to say anything you want the hiring manager to know. We keep your words as text, never your voice, because we don't want you to be nervous.
  • At the top: the company, the job title, the level, the location, and how the pay is structured.
  • A slider where you set the lowest pay you would accept.
  • Hiring managers, recruiters and teammates work as one hiring team.

    Human trials

    This button is here because we have architected, from start to finish, a human trial that we believe is important for humanity. Anybody can look at it right now and see the sense in it and the methods.

    The hypothesis

    Some people in a mental health crisis talk at length about people and moments that seem unimportant to others but clearly matter to them. Our hypothesis is that if a person's own words are recorded, with their consent, AI could find patterns in them that are actually relevant to their care, and give their professional real talking points. As far as we know, psychiatrists are not recommending this today. It is a hypothesis: an avenue to explore, not a solution.

    The trial, in brief

    • Who: people who agree to take part, with informed consent, and professionals trained in this approach.
    • What: the participant's own speech is recorded and transcribed, and AI looks for recurring themes and gives the professional talking points.
    • Measure: whether those talking points help care, as judged by the participant, the family, and the professional.
    • Safeguards: consent that can be withdrawn at any time, strict privacy for every recording, and review by an ethics board before anything starts.
