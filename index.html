Reducing human waste

Everybody gets a personal marketplace. Take a picture, press the mic, and talk about it: where you got it, how long you've had it, anything wrong with it. Pass it on, or give it away free. It isn't thrown out, and it reaches another person it will elevate.

Plan

Free hiring and recruitment for every company

Our goal is to optimize the hiring and recruitment process for every hiring manager and every company across the world, for free, forever, for both sides.

The plan
  • Add your resume and say whether you are open to work.
  • A hiring manager pastes the link to a public job posting. We show the exact search filter we will use, and it can never filter on years of experience or whether you are employed right now.
  • People whose resumes match get a link to one clean application: a yes or no next to each line of the job, and a mic to say anything you want the hiring manager to know. We keep your words as text, never your voice, because we don't want you to be nervous.
  • At the top: the company, the job title, the level, the location, and how the pay is structured.
  • A slider where you set the lowest pay you would accept.
  • Hiring managers, recruiters and teammates work as one hiring team.

Jobs we may displace. We are sorry.

If you look at our whole vision, you will see that we won't need as many people doing some of the things people do today. We are not out to eliminate jobs. We are making it so the best people at each job get to keep them, and everybody gets a better experience.
